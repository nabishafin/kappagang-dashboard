Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common creator questions

How do withdrawals work?
Withdrawals can be requested anytime with a minimum of $20. Requests are reviewed and processed within 1 business day. Bank transfers arrive in 2–3 business days. PayPal is instant with a 1.5% fee.
How is ad revenue calculated?
Ad revenue is based on CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions). Free viewers see ads while watching your videos, and you earn a share of the ad spend. Your RPM (revenue per mille) varies by content category, viewer geography, and seasonality.
What percentage of donations do I keep?
You keep 90% of every fan donation made through TipJar. The remaining 10% covers payment processing and platform infrastructure. There are no monthly fees or hidden charges.
My withdrawal was rejected — what now?
Rejections typically happen due to verification issues or mismatched payment details. Submit a support ticket with your rejection notification and we'll resolve it within 24 hours.
How do I update my payment method?
Go to Earnings & Wallet → Payment Settings to add or update your bank account, PayPal, or Stripe details. Changes take effect immediately for future withdrawals.
Why don't I see ad revenue on older videos?
Ad revenue is credited within 48–72 hours of each viewing session. If you're missing revenue, check that your content meets the ad eligibility guidelines in your account settings.
